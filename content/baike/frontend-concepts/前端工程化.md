## Monorepo

```
project/
├── packages/
│   ├── shared/       # 共享代码
│   ├── web/          # Web端
│   ├── mobile/       # 移动端
│   └── admin/        # 管理端
├── package.json
└── pnpm-workspace.yaml
```

工具：pnpm workspace, Turborepo, Nx
